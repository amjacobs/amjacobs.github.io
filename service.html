Mentorship

Identity, Belonging and Inquiry in Science (IBIS) Program Mentor

I mentored an undergraduate in research through the IBIS program at UW. The project examined how wind and temperature nudging compared to only wind nudging using ERA5 as the baseline. We found that in both case studies we examined wind and temperature nudging performs closer to ERA5 for both thermodyanmic and dynamic variables than only wind nudging. My mentee will present his findings at SACNAS and has presented at 3 different UW symposiums.
